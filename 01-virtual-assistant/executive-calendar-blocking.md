## 2. Hard Calendar Rules

1. **Mandatory 15-Minute Buffers:**  
   Every meeting automatically includes a 15-minute buffer before and after. Back-to-back calls are strictly prohibited without prior written confirmation.
2. **"No-Meeting Thursday" Afternoon:**  
   Thursday from 1:00 PM to 6:00 PM is reserved for uninterrupted focus and code/product review.
3. **Color-Coding Convention:**
   - 🔵 **Blue:** External Client & Revenue Generating Meetings
   - 🟣 **Purple:** Internal Team 1:1s and Strategy
   - 🟡 **Yellow:** Travel, Commute, Logistics Buffer
   - 🔴 **Red:** High-Stakes / Investor / Board Meetings
   - ⚪ **Grey:** Personal / Family / Hold Blocks
