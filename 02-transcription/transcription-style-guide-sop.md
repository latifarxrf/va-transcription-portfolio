# Transcription Style Guide & Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

**Prepared by:** Latifar (@latifarxrf)  
**Standard Benchmarks:** Conforms to Rev, TranscribeMe, and AHDI (Association for Healthcare Documentation Integrity) formatting standards.

---

## 1. Verbatim Modes Defined

| Category | Full Verbatim | Clean Verbatim (Default) |
|---|---|---|
| **Filler Words** (*um, uh, like, you know*) | Transcribe exactly as spoken | Omit unless grammatically load-bearing |
| **False Starts & Stutters** | Keep: *"I was— I wanted to go"* | Smooth out: *"I wanted to go"* |
| **Slang / Contractions** | Transcribe exact slang (*gonna, wanna*) | Standardize (*going to, want to*) |
| **Laughter / Non-verbal Sounds** | Tag as `[laughter]`, `[applause]` | Include only if alters conversational intent |
| **Natural Pauses** | Represent with ellipsis `...` or em-dash `—` | Use standard comma or period |

---

## 2. Punctuation, Numbers & Formatting Conventions

- **Numbers Rule:**  
  - Spell out zero through nine: *one, four, eight*.  
  - Use digits for 10 and above: *15, 250, 10,000*.  
  - Always use digits with units of measure, time, percentages, and currencies: *4 kg, 2:30 PM, 15%, $50.00*.
- **Speaker Attribution:**  
  - Insert speaker tag in bold at every speaker change:  
    `**[00:01:23] Dr. Smith:**`
- **Paragraph Length:**  
  - Keep paragraphs capped at 3–4 sentences or break whenever a distinct thematic thought begins.

---

## 3. Handling Unclear or Defective Audio

1. **Inaudible Audio:** When a word or phrase cannot be deciphered despite 3 separate replays at 0.5x speed with EQ adjustment:  
   Tag as `[inaudible HH:MM:SS]` (e.g., `[inaudible 00:04:12]`).
2. **Simultaneous Crosstalk:**  
   Attribute the dominant speaker; label cross-talk with `[crosstalk]`.
3. **Phonetic Guessing:** If reasonably confident in a word but uncertain of specific spelling:  
   Tag as `[guess? HH:MM:SS]` for reviewer secondary verification.

---

## 4. Dual-Pass Quality Assurance (QC) Protocol
- **Pass 1 (Drafting):** Capture 100% of audio dialogue using foot-pedal control, maintaining flow and timestamp precision.
- **Pass 2 (Auditing):** Continuous playback at 1.0x while reading the full text transcript. Verify spelling of proper names, medical terms, and geographical locations against authoritative web sources.
