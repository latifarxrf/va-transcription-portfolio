## 2. Key Transcription Turnaround Metrics

- **Raw Turnaround Ratio:** 3.5 : 1 (Average 21 minutes of typing per 6 minutes of standard conversational audio).
- **Dual-Pass Quality Control Time:** 1.0 : 1 (Real-time read-along review at 1.0x playback).
- **Total Delivery Velocity:** Approximately 4.5 minutes total labor per 1 minute of completed, audit-ready audio transcript.
- **Audio Hotkey Mastery:** Configured global system-wide hotkeys in Express Scribe Pro (Foot Pedal: Play/Pause/Rewind 3s) and PhraseExpress for single-stroke timestamp insertion (`[HH:MM:SS]`).

## 3. Ergonomic & Reliability Setup
- **Keyboard:** Full-size mechanical keyboard with dedicated numeric 10-key pad.
- **Audio Gear:** Closed-back Audio-Technica ATH-M50x studio headphones ensuring total acoustic isolation and zero audio bleed.
- **Typing Technique:** Strict 10-finger touch typing without looking at the keyboard, allowing eyes to remain focused on source spreadsheets and reference documentation.
